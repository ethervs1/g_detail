## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end automotive craftsmanship, technical precision, and bespoke studio detailing. Targeted at discerning vehicle enthusiasts, sports car owners, and luxury automobile collectors, the experience balances aggressive performance aesthetics with showroom elegance. 

The aesthetic is grounded in a refined, dark-mode automotive visual language:
- **Carbon & Specular Depth:** Rich deep burgundy, obsidian tones, and subtle carbon weaves layered under high-gloss specular highlight edges.
- **Precision Illumination:** Controlled ambient crimson edge-lighting reminiscent of LED tail-light lightbars and laser headlights, creating depth without visual noise.
- **Engineering Clarity:** Clean, tabular, and condensed typographic cues balanced by modern geometric structure to reflect telemetry dials, dyno readouts, and professional garage specification sheets.

## Colors

The palette directly translates the visual cues of high-performance automotive tuning and precision paint protection:

- **Primary (`#8f3d38`):** Terracotta / Brick Red. High visibility, tactile contrast. Reserved for active interactive triggers, primary calls-to-action, active stage steppers, and focus rings.
- **Secondary (`#56070c`):** Deep Crimson Accent. Used for edge gradients, active category indicators, container inner glows, and automotive badge accents.
- **Tertiary (`#350a06`):** Midnight Burgundy. Forms ambient vignettes, tinted backdrops behind card containers, and header spotlight glows.
- **Neutral Base (`#111111`):** Deep Obsidian. The foundational canvas surface that establishes absolute dark-mode contrast.
- **Supporting Grays:**
  - Cool Pewter (`#8c8c8c`): Used for secondary labels, inactive states, metric units, and subtle border highlights.
  - Steel Charcoal (`#666666`): Used for borders, divider lines, disabled UI elements, and technical specifications.
- **High-Contrast Text:** Pure White (`#ffffff`) for dominant headlines and prominent numeric figures.

## Typography

The type system balances technical motorsport aggression with clear readability:

- **Headlines & Badges (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers a technical, sharp, and machined automotive feel. Uppercase treatments with tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em` to `0.1em`) are applied to tags, category headers, and badge indicators.
- **Body & Technical Specs (`Chivo`):** Provides high legibility at dense telemetry sizes, service checklists, and warranty package descriptions. Its sturdy letterforms withstand intense dark backdrops without bleeding.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column responsive grid with controlled density, reflecting high-end automotive console interfaces:

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Service matrices and package comparisons span modular multi-column grids.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Diagnostic checklists and booking options collapse into 2-column stacked sections.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Bottom sheets, sticky action ribbons, and full-width card stacks ensure thumb-accessible booking and package inspection.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on traditional dropped shadows; instead, it uses dark-mode illumination layers, specular rim lighting, and subtle luminescence:

- **Base Layer (Ground Zero):** `#111111` solid canvas, layered with an ultra-subtle, repeating diagonal micro-weave (carbon pattern opacity: 3%).
- **Level 1 (Panels & Surfaces):** `#181818` background with a 1px border stroke (`#666666` at 25% opacity) and an inner top border reflection of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Containers):** `#1e1717` blended with a linear glow of `#56070c` (35% opacity), framed with a glowing perimeter `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(143, 61, 56, 0.25)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sticky Head-Up Triggers):** `#1a1111` with full frosted glass backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), framed by a dual border featuring a sharp rim light and a deep ambient shadow (`0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a low-radius, architectural, and aerodynamic curvature (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Corners:** Kept taut and disciplined (0.25rem to 0.5rem for standard UI elements) to invoke forged metal, precision-cut carbon fiber panels, and machined billet aluminum components.
- **Badges & Micro-Pills:** Stand out with angled cutoffs or tight radius capping to evoke race telemetry stamps and technical certifications.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Detail / Reserve Slot):** Background `#8f3d38` transitioning to `#56070c` at 135 degrees. Text in pure white (`#ffffff`), `label-lg`, Space Grotesk uppercase. Subtle red emission glow (`0 0 16px rgba(143, 61, 56, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary (Inspect Tier / Spec Sheet):** Deep matte surface (`#181818`) with 1px border (`#8c8c8c` at 40% opacity). Hover triggers border highlight (`#8f3d38`) and smooth 150ms inner red gradient bloom.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, white text, red underlines on interactive hover.

### Service & Pricing Cards
- Structured with a micro-header displaying category badges (e.g., "CERAMIC PRO", "PAINT CORRECTION").
- Card frame incorporates a 1px high-specular top border.
- Hover reveals a vertical accent stripe on the left edge (`#8f3d38`, 3px wide).

### Chips & Badges
- Compact padding (`0.25rem` vertical, `0.6rem` horizontal).
- Dark tinted background (`#350a06` at 60% opacity) coupled with `#8f3d38` 1px border.
- Text set in `label-sm` Space Grotesk uppercase with tracked lettering.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Checkbox borders set in `#666666`, transitioning to solid `#8f3d38` with an internal glowing checkmark upon selection.
- Toggles feature a black satin track and an anodized red thumb slider with a faint red specular halo.

### Input Fields
- Matte carbon-tinted fields (`#161616`) with inset dark borders (`#333333`).
- Floating placeholder labels in `#8c8c8c`.
- Focus state activates an intense 1px `#8f3d38` border and an ambient terracotta glow.

### Additional Automotive Features
- **Before / After Comparison Sliders:** Equipped with dual-handled precision needles and high-contrast division markers.
- **Vehicle Telemetry Progress Steps:** Segmented horizontal neon bars indicating detailing progress (Decontamination → Stage 2 Correction → Ceramic Curing).
